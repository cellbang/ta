## Patch Changes

- A console of `ta gateway` for its administrators, under Discover: the accounts, how many run and are connected, those waiting to be let in; the providers of sandboxes; each account's Ta with what its sandbox takes (CPU and memory, from Docker), to start, restart or stop, with its log; and a link that lets someone in, as `ta gateway link` prints. Agents too: how many work and wait across the gateway and for each account, and for each conversation of an account's Ta its status, model, messages, tool calls, tokens, cost and context, never what was said. TaGatewayServer gains getTenantAgents, getConsole, startTenant, restartTenant, getTenantLogs and createSignInLink; a provider of sandboxes may tell usage and logs (docker by its Engine API, local from its output, command by a `logs` command). (`@celljs/ta-gateway`, `@celljs/ta-harness`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-web`, `@celljs/ta`)
- ta gateway has roles, limits and an audit: owners (TA_GATEWAY_ADMINS) make admins in the console, admins manage members only; each method needs a role. Members get free minutes a month (counted by the gateway as their sandbox runs; out of them it stops until the next month or minutes given), messages a minute (past them RATE_LIMITED), pages at once, the sandboxes they may pick and whether a Ta service of their own; an administrator can suspend an account. The console has tabs (Overview, Accounts, Policy): tokens and dollars over a day, a week or a month by model and account, accounts to filter and sort with their minutes, the latest tool calls and failures of each agent, the defaults, the managers and the audit. TaUsageServer of ta serve tells what its agents took by hour and what one did lately, never what was said. (`@celljs/ta-gateway`, `@celljs/ta-host`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-web`, `@celljs/ta`)
- Ta Desktop tells of its updates: a notice once a new build is put in place (click to restart), once one could not be (with why, and Move Ta to the Applications folder when it runs where it cannot be replaced, without downloading it), and once after it restarted on a new build. (`@celljs/ta`)
