## Minor Changes

- Another coding agent gets Ta's own tools: the tools of Ta's packages and pages, the double's start_task among them, reach CodeBuddy Code and other ACP agents as an MCP server on 127.0.0.1 behind a token of the conversation, and run in Ta's turn as Ta's own. An agent can now be the double and start tasks, and is told the role of the conversation with Ta's notes. (`@celljs/ta-agents`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- Ta scrolls the screen for the user: app_do takes scroll (down or up most of a screen, top, bottom, or a row into sight), of a part or of the page's area; app_look lists only the rows in sight of what scrolls and says where it is, and whether more is loading or all is in. Parts say how they load as they scroll with useScreenPart's scroll; ui.screen has scroll and pageScroll. (`@celljs/ta-app-use`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- Ta plays games for the user: every `ui.Game` and `ui.Game3D` is a part of the screen, `game`, with its state (running, the canvas's size, the score and whether the round is over) and the actions point, move (or drag), key and hold, done by pointer, mouse and key events on its canvas and never by touch events, so no gesture of the shell starts from them. Games tell their round by `score` and `over` in the registry, or `state()` of a 3D loop, and take a `title`; app_do's cursor goes to the point of the canvas. The example games tell their score. (`@celljs/ta-app-use`, `@celljs/ta-extensions`, `@celljs/ta-games`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- A task is a conversation. The list of conversations is the double's list, first in the navigation: the double's card, the rows of its home and what waits for the user at its top, the double's tasks marked rows of it (`WebTask.session`), a team a row of its own by its time; tapping a task, anywhere, goes into its conversation or channel (`WebTask.show`). The row of the conversations going on is gone. Who the user works with is the new Partners list: friends and rooms at its top, group chats (channels and rooms), agents; rows of extensions go there by `WebHomeItem.place`. Friends and Rooms leave Discover. A Ta app of before keeps the double's sidebar. (`@celljs/ta-channels`, `@celljs/ta-manual`, `@celljs/ta-nearby`, `@celljs/ta-rooms`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- Channels and rooms are one thing to the user, a group chat. New group chat takes agents and friends: with agents alone it stays on this computer, with friends it is a room shared through the relay, the agents invited in. Friends are invited into a room (`TaRoomServer.inviteFriends`, `listGroupInvites`, `answerGroupInvite`): the invitation is sealed for each in the owner's room of friends, shows among the partners and on the page of rooms, is told once, and the friend is let in at once on joining; anyone else with the link still asks. A channel of the user's may invite friends too: a shared group chat of its name is made, its agents in it. (`@celljs/ta-channels`, `@celljs/ta-host`, `@celljs/ta-manual`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-relay`, `@celljs/ta-rooms`)
- Remote desktops over VNC through ta serve, the first stage: `TaScreenShareServer` (ta-protocol), its RFB 3.8 client in ta-host, `connection.screenShare` of ta-client, `pi.screenShare` of the page and the built-in page ta-screen-share (docs/design/ta-desktop-remote.md 13). ZRLE inflates no more than the rectangle can hold (`RfbZrle.maxBytes`) and a rectangle outside the desktop ends the connection; a desktop that does not answer while it connects is lost after 20 seconds, saying why. (`@celljs/ta-client`, `@celljs/ta-harness`, `@celljs/ta-host`, `@celljs/ta-manual`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-screen-share`, `@celljs/ta-team`, `@celljs/ta-web`, `@celljs/ta`)
- Sign in to Ta in the cloud with an account, from the app on a phone or Ta Desktop: no code to scan, no token to paste. The gateway gives a code (OAuth 2.0's device flow, `/sign-in/device`), the user signs in in a browser, or lets the device in from a browser signed in already, and the token comes to the device, which keeps the gateway among its computers. An account not let in yet waits among the requests and gets its token once let in. A build for a deployment (`TA_DISTRIBUTION`) offers its Ta in the cloud on the sign-in at once; Settings, Access lets everyone of a way to sign in in at once (`oidc:*`). `/api/files` of the gateway takes a token too. Design in docs/design/ta-deploy.md 8.1. (`@celljs/ta-gateway`, `@celljs/ta-host`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-web`, `@celljs/ta`)
- The gateway signs devices in with a company's sign-in for CLIs, which takes no address to come back to and no secret: the request is pushed to it (PAR, RFC 9126), the user agrees in a browser, and the gateway polls it for the result (`TA_IAM_ISSUER`, `TA_IAM_CLIENT_ID`; accounts `iam:<login>`). The page of a device's code offers it; the gateway's own page signs in as a device of its address. Design in docs/design/ta-deploy.md 8.2. (`@celljs/ta-gateway`, `@celljs/ta-host`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- Goals, a new built-in package: tell Ta a result you want and it drafts a card of how it is judged done; tap Start and it works on it in a conversation of its own, at its pace and within a budget a day, with no page open, asks you only when it must, and hands you the result to accept. Each goal is a task of the double, the row of its conversation; its ordinary turns tell you nothing (ta-notify knows the role mark `[ta:tells-of-its-turns]`), its questions and failures do. The manual has a topic for it. (`@celljs/ta-goals`, `@celljs/ta-harness`, `@celljs/ta-manual`, `@celljs/ta-notify`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-web`, `@celljs/ta`)
- Goals go on with less of the user: a goal's progress goes to the daily brief, a line a goal, while its ordinary turns still tell nothing at once; after 3 failed turns in a row it pauses and its row says why; Settings → Goals sets how often a goal is worked on, how long a day at most and how many at once, which a card that says nothing of it takes. The conversation of a goal may hand work to a team of agents, never does a step it must ask first on its own whatever Initiative allows, and is offered no next step to propose (ta-initiative leaves chat.next-step out of sessions that tell of their turns themselves). (`@celljs/ta-goals`, `@celljs/ta-initiative`)
- Goals wake when something happens: each has a webhook of ta serve (`/api/hooks/ta-goals/<secret>`, its address in the context of its turns) whose post is told to its next turn, and goal_update's `watch` wakes it as files of its directory change, not for what its own turn changed. Goals define the kinds `goal.set` and `goal.update` in Initiative, both only asked, and the weekly review has a line for each goal, proposing to pause or redirect one, or to hand over as a goal a result the user keeps asking for. (`@celljs/ta-goals`, `@celljs/ta-initiative`)
- Calls with a voice service tell the user from Ta's own voice heard back: a sound no louder than what plays (times bargeIn.echo) does not cut Ta off, nor do the words of it. A sentence the service ends while the user's voice was heard a moment ago (bargeIn.joinMs) goes on as one. Settings → Voice has "Speak to cut in": off, only a tap stops Ta (bargeIn.tap). ta-voice ships its host built with semantic VAD for OpenAI Realtime. (`@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-voice`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- Every build offers the company accounts of a Ta in the cloud on its sign-in: apps/ta/distribution.json names its gateway, used when TA_DISTRIBUTION names none (`none` for no deployment). "Use another company's Ta in the cloud" takes the address of another, kept on the device and offered first from then on. Out of reach of the gateway, the sign-in is as it was. (`@celljs/ta-web`, `@celljs/ta`)

## Patch Changes

- A call's front never runs on another coding agent, and no conversation that may use only some of Ta's tools does. With the conversation on CodeBuddy Code, the front of a call took its fast model (haiku): the agent searched and read files with its own tools while every call of it failed in Ta with "Tool agent_tool not found", and nothing was said. The settings now list the models of other agents (TaSettingsSnapshot.agents, their api beginning with TA_AGENT_API_PREFIX); the front takes a fast model of Ta instead, else another of Ta. And ta-agents refuses a session whose role took agent_tool away with the rest of the tools (a call's front, a turn for others in a room, triage), saying to pick a model of Ta, rather than letting the agent work unbounded. (`@celljs/ta-agents`, `@celljs/ta-call`, `@celljs/ta-host`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`)
- Ta's work on the screen costs less and moves like a hand: an older sight of the screen is cut from what the model is sent, a step says only what changed, places by their names, app_do finds by words and goes there first in one step, and each step waits for the page to settle rather than for the slowest; the cursor swings along an arc in a time that grows with the way, a touch lands and lifts on a phone, the ring keeps to what it rings, the line beside it never leaves the screen, and the pill and cursor stay while the work goes on. (`@celljs/ta-app-use`)
- What Ta reads of a part that scrolls is what the user sees: only its rows drawn and in sight, numbered among those drawn, with how many are drawn and whether more come; a row not drawn yet says to scroll toward it. (`@celljs/ta-app-use`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- A question to the user over a conversation is read by Ta wherever the conversation is scrolled: items outside what a part scrolls say fixed: true and are always listed (p1.f1). (`@celljs/ta-app-use`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- Ta works the user's own screen first, as their shadow: the page they use, on a phone a phone's, on a computer a computer's, with a cursor (a touch on a phone) and a pill to stop it, waiting while their finger is on the page; a call goes on as Ta takes them to another page. Ta's own screen is for work apart (apart: true). (`@celljs/ta-app-use`, `@celljs/ta-call`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- The call going on is a part of the screen over every page: app_look lists it first wherever the page is (what it is at, with which conversation, captions on or off), and app_do presses its own buttons: hang up, interrupt, done, captions, back to the call. A call on one device of the user now shows on their others too, at the top of the page, where it goes on, with Hang up. useScreenPart takes floats for a part over every page: listed first, never what the page scrolls. (`@celljs/ta-app-use`, `@celljs/ta-extensions`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- The double tells how a task ended in a sentence or two, without repeating the numbers, files or comparisons the task already shows; a bot's answer is one or two short paragraphs, the answer first. (`@celljs/ta-bots`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- What was said is read in one place: TaSaid of ta-protocol (words, of, line, marksOnly; leaks and SAMPLES for tests) gives what a person reads of a message, without the marks for the model (the context of a message, pi's notes of images, the skill block, the notes of the double's tasks, the summary an agent compacts its context to, lines of a tag alone), the cards of Ta's pages left out where nothing draws them. The list of conversations, notices, links shared, bots, the titles of Usage, copies and exports, what is read aloud, recall, the export of a channel's agent, the front of a call and the text put back on a fork read it, and no package writes or reads a mark by hand any more (TaMessageContext.wrap, TaMessageSource.line); `bun run check:marks` keeps it so. Fixed on the way: a link shared carried the context and the skill files of the user's messages, a bot sent summaries and the JSON of cards to WeChat, Usage could title a conversation `<context>`, a reply of a card alone showed `ta-glance {…}` in the list. (`@celljs/ta-bots`, `@celljs/ta-call`, `@celljs/ta-channels`, `@celljs/ta-cron`, `@celljs/ta-host`, `@celljs/ta-initiative`, `@celljs/ta-notify`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-recall`, `@celljs/ta-share`, `@celljs/ta-usage`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- Ask Ta to hang up in a call and it does, after a goodbye (`hang_up` of a call's front; `WebCallSession.ending`). (`@celljs/ta-call`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- The model of calls is picked among Ta's models only: `ui.ModelPicker` takes `noAgents`, which leaves out other coding agents (TaSettingsSnapshot.agents), and the settings of calls use it; an agent picked there before shows as the default, which is what a call takes. (`@celljs/ta-call`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- A call's conversation is named by the first sentence the user said in it (Call · what they said), so calls are told apart in the list, and it is archived as the user's other conversations are, no longer 10 minutes after the call ended, so the Calls folder stays in the list. (`@celljs/ta-call`, `@celljs/ta-extensions`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- Answering for the user (the action asks.answer) waits for them to agree on their screen: it could answer a confirmation of another conversation (delete, publish) with only the model's word that the user said so. It is a danger action now, and its confirmation says which conversation, what it asks and the answer (TaAppCommand.detail, TaPageConfirm.detail); an answer that cannot be one is refused before the user is asked, and a yes or no reads 是 / 否, not 开 / 关. (`@celljs/ta-call`, `@celljs/ta-host`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`)
- What the user said and what a part of Ta tells are told apart: a message that is not the user's (news of the work in a call, a scheduled run's time limit, a permission granted) is a notice, `[ta:notice …]` (TaMessageSource.notice), and every session is told in `<ta-sources>` to weigh it below what the user said, never as their request. The pages draw a notice as a quiet line with where it came from, opened by a tap, not as the user's bubble, and lists neither title a conversation by it nor show it as what was said last. In a call, what the front said of news is not read aloud once the user spoke since: their words come first. The `[news]` prefix of calls is gone. (`@celljs/ta-call`, `@celljs/ta-cron`, `@celljs/ta-host`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-team`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- The amber dot of something unseen is on the entry of the double on the rail of a computer too, as on the first tab of a phone, and drags away as a drop to see all; the dot of a row drags away too, seeing that conversation (a folder's, all of it). Calls are left off the entry: web extensions' chat icons get `quiet`, whose dot stays on its row, and `archiveMinutes`, which archives a conversation that many minutes after it was last updated; the conversations of a call are both, archived 10 minutes after it ended. (`@celljs/ta-call`, `@celljs/ta-extensions`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- What waits for the user to decide is on the double's home: each conversation that asks is a row of its own under Waiting for you, above the tasks, with a blue ?, and the entry of the double counts them; the chats no longer have a section of their own at the top nor a blue count on their tab. (`@celljs/ta-channels`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- Tasks of the double that are no conversation are archived with the conversations once they ended before the bound of the archive, and a task whose conversation is archived is no row of its own anymore. Keeping an agent Ta made for a task now says "Save as an agent", and where it went: under Agents in Partners. (`@celljs/ta-channels`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- A goal shows running only while a turn of it goes on: between its turns it is at rest (a new task status, `idle`), so the double no longer spins "1 running" with nothing at work. (`@celljs/ta-channels`, `@celljs/ta-goals`, `@celljs/ta-town`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- Agents have 25 faces to pick from, nine more: a shield, a house, a trapezoid, a pill, a lantern, a cloud, a jar, a gourd and a frog, in the colours of the faces before. ta-channels draws its faces from FaceUtil.art of ta-relay as ui.Face does, no longer from a copy of its own, and the shirts of Ta Town follow the same faces. (`@celljs/ta-channels`, `@celljs/ta-relay`, `@celljs/ta-town`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- The tasks under the double share one tile: the conversations going on were a round green tile of their own, larger and of a thinner stroke, beside the grey rounded squares of the double's tasks. Every list now draws a task by `ui.TaskIcon`: who does it (`WebTask.icon`), else what it is (`WebTask.glyph`, a list without it) on a rounded square whose corners and glyph grow with its side, green while it goes on and grey once ended. (`@celljs/ta-channels`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- Suggestions take ideas: a package brings some in `pi.ideas` of its package.json, Initiative offers those of the packages that are on, two at a time and each once, under "Things you could hand to Ta" (kind `idea.try`), and Try it opens a conversation in which Ta asks what it needs first. Scheduled tasks, Goals and Recall bring the first three. The user changes how much Ta does on its own in words, through the actions `initiative.mode` and `initiative.level`. A prompt of a proposal accepted is sent once the book's work is done, as words to a conversation are. (`@celljs/ta-cron`, `@celljs/ta-goals`, `@celljs/ta-initiative`, `@celljs/ta-recall`)
- A folder of the list pinned (`registerChatIcon` `folder`, as Calls) no longer goes away once all of its conversations are archived: it stays, and lists the archived ones when opened. The archive folds the conversations of a folder into one row too. (`@celljs/ta-extensions`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- What Ta keeps for the user (the daily brief, goals, scheduled tasks, suggestions) is pinned to the top of the list of chats the first time it is there, and the user may unpin it, as any conversation: the rows of `registerHomeItems` are rows of the list, pinned or by their time, as the folders of `registerChatIcon` are, both pinned once (`chat.pinOffered`, the same on every device). A row of the home may give the `sessionFile` it stands for, which the list then does not list again: the daily brief is one row, no longer a row under the double and its pinned conversation too. ta-recall no longer pins its conversation itself. (`@celljs/ta-extensions`, `@celljs/ta-recall`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- A card can ask for several choices (`"multi": true` on an options block) or several questions at once (an options block each): the user picks in each, the recommended ones to begin with, and sends them together with one button. (`@celljs/ta-glance`)
- A card that waits on choices no longer says "Needs you" at its top: the ? of its question says it, and stops once answered. (`@celljs/ta-glance`)
- The choices of a card that asks several things are quiet rows with a check mark, as a list of settings, instead of a box and a button each. (`@celljs/ta-glance`)

- A build for a deployment offers the ways to sign in of its gateway first on its sign-in, as "Continue with <company account>": one tap opens the browser at that way for the device's code, no address to type and no code to check.
