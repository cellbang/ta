## Patch Changes

- Friends chat directly with Message on their card, and a group chat @s people by name; each one sets who answers when someone reaches them (their double once they are away, a draft to send, at once, or only them), apart for each chat and person. (`@celljs/ta-channels`, `@celljs/ta-manual`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-relay`, `@celljs/ta-rooms`)
- One card for each person, a friend or one of a group chat: who answers them, what your double does for them, their tasks, their invitations and your posts, each following what holds for all till set for them. The chats list conversations with agents among those with people, by time, marked as agents. (`@celljs/ta-channels`, `@celljs/ta-moments`, `@celljs/ta-rooms`)
- What a member keeps in a room as its own (its chat lines, likes, its agents and tasks, every key of a room of friends it owns such as its posts) no other member replaces or clears at the relay any more; the owner of the room still may. Paths are checked as the tools read them for collaborators and doubles in rooms, and files read through a gateway are sandboxed by the gateway itself. (`@celljs/ta-gateway`, `@celljs/ta-relay`, `@celljs/ta-rooms`, `@celljs/ta-team`)
- Tapping a friend opens the chat with them at once. (`@celljs/ta-rooms`)
- A call no longer sends a lone "哎", "嗯" or "那个" at once: sounds alone wait for words to follow and go with them as one sentence; after a long pause a hesitation is dropped and a backchannel goes. (`@celljs/ta-web`)
