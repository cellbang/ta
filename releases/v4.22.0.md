## Minor Changes

- Feedback in a group chat, the second time: bugs fixed at once and wishes on the owner's word (or a plan first); each turn of a fix ends with finish_fix, is checked without the user's secrets, and goes on in the same conversation when the owner answers or asks for changes; the room is told at each key moment. What any app of a group chat needs is the rooms': reading it paced (RoomPacer), who speaks (roomSpeaker), a board every member and browser guest sees (RelayRoomBoard); who comes in (the rule guests), how many and how long a link works are set in Manage the group chat. Work for others (a fix, a double's turn or task for someone else) is confined by ta serve (TA_CONFINED_MARK, ConfineExtension), and a package carries on its own sessions after a restart (resumeByOpener). (`@celljs/ta-feedback`, `@celljs/ta-host`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-relay`, `@celljs/ta-rooms`)

## Patch Changes

- Add members of a group chat of agents lists friends too: picking one turns it into a group chat with them in place, with no separate Invite friends. (`@celljs/ta-channels`)
- The user manual opens with who does what: you tell your double, its tasks are conversations, a group chat of your agents, and friends with their doubles; the topic of agents says group chat where it said channel. (`@celljs/ta-manual`)
