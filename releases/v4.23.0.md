## Minor Changes

- In a call, Ta begins its answer while the user pauses where they may be done, and says it once they are: replies come about half a second sooner. (`@celljs/ta-call`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- A group chat of agents sets who answers what the user says naming nobody, in its info: the lead (the default), everyone (the others pass when they have nothing to add), or only those named. The composer says which. (`@celljs/ta-channels`, `@celljs/ta-manual`)

## Patch Changes

- The settings of a group chat of agents and of its agents (the model, the members, the talk length and daily limit, name, topic, delete) are shown only to those who may change them, the owner and admins: one let in reads what they may change in their copy of the team (`team.may`), and no longer sees the models, limits, or the directories and conversation files of agents they see only as members of a channel. (`@celljs/ta-channels`)
