## Minor Changes

- Ta for school (`@celljs/ta-edu`, an industry package that comes with Ta, off until the user picks Education by the question of the field above the double's message box, in Industries of the market or in Settings → Extensions; a built-in package is off by default with `pi.offByDefault`): an assistant each for students, parents, teachers and class teachers of primary and secondary school, quiz cards answered in the reply with spaced review of the mistakes, learning cards, and skills for guided tutoring, questions, marking, lesson plans, parents and class work. (`@celljs/ta-edu`, `@celljs/ta-extensions`, `@celljs/ta-harness`, `@celljs/ta-manual`, `@celljs/ta`)
- A tone with each reminder, turned on in Settings → Notifications → Sound (off at first): web pages with no notices of the system told of answers by nothing. (`@celljs/ta-notify`, `@celljs/ta-web`)

## Patch Changes

- The tools of a tab of the workbench go in its head beside the tabs (`ui.EditorActions`), as the actions of an editor's title in an IDE: a file no longer draws a row of its own over what it shows that only names the file again with a switch of two buttons. Preview and edit are one icon button there, save another; `openEditor` takes a `tooltip` (the path of a file, under a pointer on its tab). (`@celljs/ta-extensions`, `@celljs/ta-web`, `@celljs/ta-workspace`)
- Share a file from the explorer: Share… in the menu of a file opens the share dialog with its name and size, who may open it, for how long and whether a sealed copy stays on the relay, then its link and QR code (8 MB at most, an `ssh://` workspace too). Web extensions get `registerFileMenu`, entries of the menu of a file or a folder of a list of files, and `ui.fileMenu`, what they add, for such a list to show. (`@celljs/ta-extensions`, `@celljs/ta-share`, `@celljs/ta-web`, `@celljs/ta-workspace`)
- What feedback and the double of a room tell the model goes in the context of the message; its words are what the user reads, in their language and drawn as Markdown (the new lines of a group chat numbered, a report with its title, what the asker wrote). A message the user typed still shows as typed. (`@celljs/ta-feedback`, `@celljs/ta-relay`, `@celljs/ta-rooms`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- Shared content opens on a domain of its own (`TA_CONTENT_ORIGIN`), never the relay's: links of before are sent there, and a page anyone reports or blocks takes down that domain alone. Anyone reports a link from its page; the operator reads reports and blocks a host or a share at `/v1/moderation` with `TA_ADMIN_TOKEN`. ta-share makes public links of services of this computer (`public_link`, a Quick Tunnel of Cloudflare), off until the user turns them on in My shares, never where `distribution.json` says `"publicTunnels": false` (TA_PUBLIC_TUNNELS=off). (`@celljs/ta-harness`, `@celljs/ta-host`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-relay`, `@celljs/ta-share`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- The operator of the relay handles reports in Ta: the token kept in the vault, a notice for each new report, and a page of reports to look at what was reported (when its link was sent with it), block the link or the computer that shared it, dismiss, and unblock. A report sends the link itself when the one who reports lets it. (`@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-relay`, `@celljs/ta-secrets`, `@celljs/ta-share`)
