## Minor Changes

- Education works with no page open: the scheduled tasks of each identity, a student's week sent each Sunday by the server, and a pupil of primary school let in by a parent's yes in their own Ta. (`@celljs/ta-edu`)
- Results shown by evidence: a goal may say what must not get worse (`keep`), and its result is taken only when each still holds, checked again; a bug fixed from feedback comes with its test, which Ta runs on the commit the fix started from (it must fail) and on the fix (it must pass), or says why no test can show it. (`@celljs/ta-feedback`, `@celljs/ta-goals`)
- Quiet hours and the right moment: in quiet hours (Settings → Notifications) only what is urgent comes at once and the rest is told of together after, opening the brief; answers during a pause wait instead of being dropped; `notify_user` takes `at`, a reminder for when it is time to act, and `urgent`. (`@celljs/ta-notify`)

## Patch Changes

- A video call no longer goes dark or flashes as she changes moves: the next clip is decoded behind and fades in over the one shown only once its first frame is on the screen, the one under it whole until the fade ends; a change asked for meanwhile waits, the clip to come next is decoded ahead, the fade is short at the seated seam and longer the farther her pose, one place decides what plays, and her mouth fades with the clips. What she can do is in one place and told to Ta with the words that bring it, both when Ta answers and when the voice service does; asked for a move she has not, she says so. The new built-in `@celljs/ta-moves` adds the user's own clips (Settings, Moves in video calls): checked and made like hers with ffmpeg, their ends blended into her seated when far from it, framed when she stands, played before her own and in place of one of the same name, hers again when a clip cannot play; `registerCallMoves` is the API, and ta-lips draws her mouth on them too. (`@celljs/ta-call`, `@celljs/ta-harness`, `@celljs/ta-lips`, `@celljs/ta-moves`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- A video call makes the moves Ta says it makes: a heart, a wave, clapping, laughing, a dance (turning around) or a (`@celljs/ta-call`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
  stretch, as the sentence that names one is heard, played whole and back to rest, hurried back when the call goes on.
  Whoever answers is told the moves there are from one place (TaCallMoves), and says plainly when one is not. A page that
  stayed open across an install of Ta loads again into the new build.
- Discover and the settings are sorted again by what the user looks for. Discover gets a card for Ta itself (Extensions, (`@celljs/ta-call`, `@celljs/ta-edu`, `@celljs/ta-extensions`, `@celljs/ta-games`, `@celljs/ta-goals`, `@celljs/ta-host`, `@celljs/ta-initiative`, `@celljs/ta-lips`, `@celljs/ta-manual`, `@celljs/ta-moves`, `@celljs/ta-notify`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-recall`, `@celljs/ta-rooms`, `@celljs/ta-screen-share`, `@celljs/ta-secrets`, `@celljs/ta-sync`, `@celljs/ta-town`, `@celljs/ta-usage`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
  Usage, Remote desktops, the manual) and one for play (Games, Town); a package started on open says its card with
  `"group"` in `pi`. The settings get three parts of their own: Chats (the list, notices, who answers others, links,
  storage), Initiative & goals, and Voice & calls (with Moves and Mouth, shorter titles); Education moves to Extensions,
  Device sync and the Vault come first in their parts, and an extension's section can set its `order`.
- In a video call she looks like herself as she talks: the lips drawn take the colour of hers, and none are drawn over her hand or her wide smile. Asked to dance or stretch, Ta says it will and does, rather than saying it cannot. (`@celljs/ta-call`, `@celljs/ta-lips`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- In a group chat the choices an agent offers in its card can be tapped where they are: the choice goes into the chat as the user's, naming that agent (`@name`), so it goes on in the group chat rather than in its own conversation; the card shows the choice made once the user answered. `ui.Markdown` takes `send` and `answer` for that, as a conversation gives its code blocks. - In a video call her mouth is drawn for every frame of the clip, 24 a second rather than 12: a computer that cannot draw as many draws every second or third and the frames between from the two around them, each smoothed with its neighbours so that it jitters less; when Ta's voice stops short, the frames of what was heard go out at once, and when the drawing falls behind it goes on from what will show rather than drawing what was missed. (`@celljs/ta-lips`)
- A video call can draw her own mouth: a model trained on her own videos and on Ta's voice, which looks like her and follows the words better. It is on for a new download, and in Settings for one before it (about 1 GB more, once). (`@celljs/ta-lips`)
