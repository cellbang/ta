## Minor Changes

- A secret of the vault can be kept to a directory marked work or private: elsewhere Ta is not told its name, a command naming it is refused, git gets no token of it. Keys sent or asked for in such a conversation are kept to it from the start; with its mark gone, it is given to no one. Before a conversation of work or private sends what it holds to a group chat, someone else's Ta or people nearby, Ta asks once (TA_OUTBOUND_TOOLS). (`@celljs/ta-boundaries`, `@celljs/ta-host`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-secrets`)
- A guest talks with Ta in a home of their own, where Ta knows nothing of the owner: ta serve confines every conversation there and says it speaks with a guest (TaGuestUtil), whichever packages are on; the role Guest may start conversations and keep files there, no command, no folder of the owner's. Its boundary is guest: no profile, no recall, no secret, nothing sent out. The owner's list folds the guests' conversations into one row. (`@celljs/ta-boundaries`, `@celljs/ta-host`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-secrets`, `@celljs/ta-team`)
- Creator, an industry that comes with Ta and is off by default: a channel without showing your face. Hot topics of your field found three times a day (news while it is hot, how-tos, good work abroad explained with its source), each piece made on this computer (code typed in, the voice of its speech, captions and the AI label, a version for each platform you pick), reviewed in one card on your phone and filled in for you in your browser up to Publish, and the numbers reviewed each Monday. (`@celljs/ta-creator`, `@celljs/ta-extensions`, `@celljs/ta-harness`)
- A block of the earth is about a metre: the grid of the earth is of zoom 25 (a plot 512 columns across), a block a metre high, so that buildings, roads and rivers are as large as they are beside one. Trees, doors, where one comes in and Ta's building go by metres; the map seen from far is made at half the columns. What was built before stays where it was, made finer as it is read (EarthFiner), in an island and in a world played together. GeoRaster.plot takes the columns across; VoxelKey spans twice as many chunks. (`@celljs/ta-earth`, `@celljs/ta-geo`, `@celljs/ta-voxel`)
- Ta's building: the nearest building one goes into holds what the user has in Ta (desks for the tasks at work, a board of what waits for them, a clock for the scheduled tasks, a meeting table for the call, a tower of goals on the roof), each opened by aiming at it. Ta's people walk, type and fly with arms and legs that move. Every face of a block is drawn with a tile of its own (grass, bark, bricks, shingles…), from an atlas the engine makes of the tiles a world paints. (`@celljs/ta-earth`, `@celljs/ta-voxel`)
- Block Earth has the real weather of the place (its sunrise and sunset, grey skies, rain and snow, fog, storms, snow on the ground, how warm), the ground as high as it is there, and buildings to walk into, storeys with a ladder up to the roof; ta-geo reads the heights of the ground and the weather (GeoElevation, GeoWeather), ta-voxel climbs ladders. (`@celljs/ta-earth`, `@celljs/ta-geo`, `@celljs/ta-voxel`)
- A fix that lacks what only those who reported it know asks them itself (finish_fix with need_info): through the relay (`@celljs/ta-feedback`, `@celljs/ta-relay`)
  for feedback of the inbox, with @ in the group chat for a room's, twice at most. What they answer (on the thread of
  their feedback, or in the room) goes back to the fix, which carries on. The sender of a feedback comments on it at once,
  without the wait of new accounts, so that they can answer.
- Cards draw charts laid out in a grid (`board`): the nine palaces, the twelve houses round a middle, the four pillars, the six lines of a hexagram, a seating plan, each cell with a name, a tag and a few short lines, the one that matters drawn apart, in light and dark and on a phone. (`@celljs/ta-glance`)

## Patch Changes

- A fourth review of the extension API of Ta. A stopped extension's `ui.startGroup` no longer opens the picker. Block Earth reads the agents of ta-channels from its host's `listAgents`, which now marks those answering in a channel `busy`, not from its data. The page of Ta's own screen tells a phone from a computer by the root's `data-handheld`, as the shell does, not by a width of its own; the Usage page lays out by `data-layout`. Where the web client keeps the pages used last is `TA_PAGES_USED` of ta-protocol, which ta-initiative reads. The README of ta-web (en, zh) and the ta-package skill list the APIs added since the last review; the skill gives the four languages of `ui.language`. (`@celljs/ta-app-use`, `@celljs/ta-channels`, `@celljs/ta-earth`, `@celljs/ta-extensions`, `@celljs/ta-initiative`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-usage`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- Say it in a sentence: "this project is for the company" marks the directory (the action boundaries.mark), "use opus in this project" sets the model of that project alone (settings.set with scope project); each says what changed, with Undo. The tag on top of a conversation opens what it means and how to change it. (`@celljs/ta-app-use`, `@celljs/ta-boundaries`, `@celljs/ta-host`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- One look across the pages of Ta: the buttons, fields and empty state of Moves, the tabs of Usage and of the changes of a reply as the segmented control, the switch of Education, the buttons of a suggestion at 32px, and the help of ta gateway at the width of the others. (`@celljs/ta-app-use`, `@celljs/ta-changes`, `@celljs/ta-edu`, `@celljs/ta-gateway`, `@celljs/ta-initiative`, `@celljs/ta-moments`, `@celljs/ta-moves`, `@celljs/ta-rooms`, `@celljs/ta-secrets`, `@celljs/ta-usage`, `@celljs/ta-web`, `@celljs/ta`)
- An agent is busy to other packages (listAgents) as the side bar shows it: answering in a channel or in its own conversation, and waiting when its conversation waits for the user to answer a dialog. On the island of ta-earth, an agent the user talks with directly works at its desk, no longer idle. (`@celljs/ta-channels`, `@celljs/ta-earth`)
- Fly up to look down on the world: jumps in the air no longer land (coming down to the ground does), the phone shows Up and Down while flying, climbing is as fast as flying, more of the world is made around and the fog keeps beyond the ground below when high, up to 120 blocks over the ground. (`@celljs/ta-earth`, `@celljs/ta-voxel`)
- Jumping is only jumping: Fly / Land (Q) takes off and lands. Flying goes up to 400 blocks, at a speed chosen (0.5× to 8×, + and −) and faster the higher, the ground far off drawn from the map; seen from the eyes or from behind (V); the map in the corner opens big (or M), dragged and zoomed, and a place tapped is gone to at once. (`@celljs/ta-earth`, `@celljs/ta-voxel`)
- "Where I am" in Block Earth asks for the location and never hangs: the phone and desktop apps may ask for it, and a refusal or no answer is said in words. (`@celljs/ta-earth`, `@celljs/ta-geo`)
- Buildings look more like themselves: houses with roofs of steps rising to the middle, tall ones flat with a parapet and machines on top, corners without windows, a lamp over the door and a ground storey of glass; inside, lights in the ceilings and furniture along the walls (shelves, tables, sofas, plants, beds in houses), kept clear of the door, the ladder and the things of Ta's building. (`@celljs/ta-earth`)
- The earth is lit by its sun: it rises in the east, stands to the south at noon and sets in the west by the hour of the place, the moon dim and blue across the sky at night, casting soft shadows round the user (nearer and coarser on a phone) of the blocks, the people and the furniture. The sky is a dome blue overhead and pale to the horizon, glowing round the sun; the haze of the distance takes the horizon's colour, warm in the morning and evening; cloud fades the shadows. Grass, leaves, roads and water are softer and warmer; signs, screens and name labels keep their colours. VoxelMeshes takes `lit` (drawn by the lights of the scene, VoxelMesher giving normals and leaving out the shade of each side) and `shadows`. (`@celljs/ta-earth`, `@celljs/ta-voxel`)
- Ta's building looks its own: glass from floor to ceiling between white slabs, green along them and up its corners, a garden with trees on its roof, white floors and ceilings of light panels; its ground storey laid out in order (the board and the clock at the front, desks in rows facing them, the meeting table at the back, big plants), and a booth for every page of Ta, by its side walls and on the storey above, each opening its page. Storeys of every building are four blocks high. (`@celljs/ta-earth`)
- Fix the stick and A/D stepping to the wrong side when facing east or west: the body now steps to the camera's right. (`@celljs/ta-earth`, `@celljs/ta-voxel`)
- A card of a reply (`ta-glance`) in a group chat, seen from a browser (the page of guests of the relay, and the viewer of a link without Ta's page), is drawn as a card, read only, rather than as its JSON.   and a full-width colon or comma between values no longer leave the whole card undrawn.
