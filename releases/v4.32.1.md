## Patch Changes

- The directory of public places refuses a listing signed without its time, and lets go of the pictures of reports it no longer keeps; the browser and the voice of Creator end on a failure instead of leaving a browser running or waiting for the time to run out. (`@celljs/ta-creator`, `@celljs/ta-relay`)
- A card in a reply knows the conversation it is in (`WebCodeRendererProps.conversation`); a piece of Creator asked for review in a conversation is answered there when passed on the page, so that conversation no longer waits for the user to decide. (`@celljs/ta-creator`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- Creator's page is laid out anew: the way a piece goes (topics, making, review, published, numbers) as stations on top, each with its count and what goes on, and the station picked below with room to read. A channel runs several series at once (a course numbers its lessons and goes on from the last), topics, pieces in the making and pieces to review can be removed and put back, a piece passed is published by Ta or filled in up to Publish as set, the making of a piece is drawn as its six steps, and the numbers of each piece are compared by series, kind, form and platform, what a review concludes steering the topics to come. (`@celljs/ta-creator`)
- A link of a share opened in the workbench has two tools in the head of its tab: copy the link, and open it in the browser. (`@celljs/ta-share`)
- A session that works for others or on what they wrote (a fix of feedback, a group chat) no longer gets team_audit, which its own package refused. (`@celljs/ta-team`)
- Calls of the tools that work a computer or a browser (Cua Driver, Playwright: click, fill in, press a key, look at a window, take a screenshot, open an app) read in words, whatever server they come from, not as their names (`click`, `Set value`); an MCP tool named in one word reads as a word too. (`@celljs/ta-web`)
