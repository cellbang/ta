## Minor Changes

- Meetings, a new package of Ta on by default: a meeting is written down as it is said (text only, kept on this computer as long and as much as Settings → Voice → Meetings says), and at the end Ta gives the minutes, the key points and the to-dos in a conversation of its own, then does the to-dos the user picks. Web extensions write down speech until stopped with `ui.voice.transcribe`, through the voice service of Settings → Voice when one is chosen, else the recognizer of the device. (`@celljs/ta-harness`, `@celljs/ta-meeting`, `@celljs/ta-web`)

## Patch Changes

- An inbox of Ta (`host.inbox`): what wakes a conversation without the user is kept on disk till it went, one at a time a conversation, what comes meanwhile sent together after its turn; follow-ups, limit resets, goals, the weekly review and the daily brief are its timers. Scheduled tasks can tell only of news (`nothing_new`), and the page of scheduled tasks has Ta's schedule: what wakes Ta next and what woke it lately. Runs nobody watches take no step hard to undo (ta-permission-gate, now built in). The double's ask before a task no longer holds its turn: the answer comes back as a message. A goal goes on in a new conversation, with its plan, log and notes, once the context fills or was compacted. (`@celljs/ta-cron`, `@celljs/ta-goals`, `@celljs/ta-harness`, `@celljs/ta-host`, `@celljs/ta-initiative`, `@celljs/ta-permission-gate`, `@celljs/ta-protocol`, `@celljs/ta-recall`, `@celljs/ta-web`)
- Ta comes back to a conversation later by itself: `schedule_task` has `follow_up`, `follow_ups` and `cancel_follow_up`, which wake the same conversation at a time with a note (to wait for CI, a deploy or a reply rather than sleep in a turn). A scheduled run is told, out of sight, what the run before told the user, so it says what is new; and when a step needs the user it asks at the end instead of guessing, the conversation waiting for their answer. (`@celljs/ta-cron`, `@celljs/ta-manual`)
